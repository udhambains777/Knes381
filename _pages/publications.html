--- layout: archive title: "About Me" permalink: /publications/ author_profile: true --- I am a 4th year Kinesiology student at the University of Calgary with a clear sense of where I am headed. My goal is to become a pediatrician, and everything I have done throughout my undergraduate years has been shaped by that ambition. ## Why Pediatrics Working with children has never felt like an obligation to me; it has always come naturally. There is something deeply rewarding about being in a space where you can genuinely make a difference in a child's development, confidence, and wellbeing. The more time I spend around kids in different settings, the more certain I become that pediatric medicine is where I belong. That conviction shows up across nearly every role I have taken on. As a volunteer with Children's Adapted Physical Activity, I support children with physical, mental, and verbal disabilities in adapted movement programs, helping create an environment where every child feels safe, included, and encouraged to participate. Through PEMRAP, I had the opportunity to work at the Alberta Children's Hospital, contributing to research that directly impacts pediatric populations. Even in my job at Kumon, I work one-on-one with children, helping them build skills in math and English, and stepping into a leadership role whenever the manager is absent. Across all of these experiences, the common thread is simple: I genuinely enjoy working with kids, and I am good at it. ## Research and Academics Beyond my work with children, I have developed a strong interest in human movement and biomechanics. As a member of the Mechatronics Integration of Neural Dynamics (MIND) club, I contribute to research on neuroprosthetic devices and brain-computer interface technologies, applying my understanding of motor learning and human movement to help improve the precision of assistive devices. I also volunteered with ACSCON Ambulosono, where I gained hands-on experience in data collection, lab communication, and the care of wearable research equipment. ## Outside of School When I am not studying or volunteering, I like to keep things simple. I am someone who can easily lose an entire weekend to a good show; there is nothing quite like finding a new series and committing to it completely. I also love getting out with friends, whether that is grabbing food, exploring the city, or just spending time together. Reading is another escape for me, particularly when I find something that pulls me in and makes me think. I believe that balance is what keeps everything else sustainable, and I try to bring that same mindset into everything I do.
